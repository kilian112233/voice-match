random thought but emily dickinson is honestly one of the wildest cases in poetry. lived basically her whole life in one house in amherst. barely left. and while she was alive she published like nothing, a handful of poems nobody read. then she died and her sister found this whole treasure hoard of poems in her room. changed everything.

her style is weird at first. all the dashes. random capital letters. but thats the point i guess. she crams these huge questions into these tiny little poems. "because i could not stop for death, he kindly stopped for me". death just pulls up like a taxi. i've read it a bunch of times and it still gets me.

the contrast is what i cant get over. she barely left her dads house but her head was everywhere. her letters are actually pretty funny, really sharp, she knew all the big ideas of her time. i think she picked the tiny life on purpose so she could think bigger. idk. something like that.

and the punchline is kind of ironic. she hid her poems her whole life and now 200 years later everyone reads them. at first people laughed at her stuff, too weird, too rough, they called it unpolished and eccentric, didnt get what she was doing with those dashes and that weird capitalization, and now its basically untouchable. guess the quiet ones are the ones worth listening to.
